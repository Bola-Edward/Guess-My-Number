# Guess My Number

A simple browser game where you try to find a randomly selected number between 1 and 20. The game gives you higher-or-lower hints and tracks your score as you play.

## How to Play

1. Enter a number between **1 and 20**.
2. Select **Check!** to see whether your guess is correct.
3. Use the higher-or-lower hints to guide your next guess. Each incorrect guess reduces your score by one.
4. Guess correctly before your score reaches zero to win. Your score at the time of the win becomes your high score if it beats the previous one.
5. Select **Again!** to start a new round. Your score resets to 20, a new secret number is chosen, and your high score is kept for the current page session.
